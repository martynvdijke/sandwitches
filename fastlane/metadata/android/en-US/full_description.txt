Sandwitches is a native Android client for a self-hosted Sandwitches recipe server.

Browse and search sandwich recipes, read ingredients and step-by-step instructions, rate recipes, keep favourites, place orders and follow their progress. The app talks to your own Sandwitches instance, so your recipes and account stay on hardware you control.

Features:
<ul>
<li>Browse the community recipe collection</li>
<li>Step-by-step cooking mode and an e-ink friendly reading view</li>
<li>Ratings, reviews and favourites</li>
<li>Shopping cart and order tracking</li>
<li>Sign in against your own server</li>
<li>Works with any Sandwitches server URL, including a local development instance</li>
</ul>

You need access to a Sandwitches server to use this app. The server is free software released under the MIT licence and lives in the same source repository as the app.
